## Selected work

One brand I manage, and how I approach it.

**The challenge.** Mortgages are confusing, and the broker prefers not to show his face on social media. The content has to feel personal without a person on camera.

**What I do.** I plan the monthly content calendar, design carousels and text-led posts, write captions in plain language, and review reach, saves, and follower growth to decide what to post next.
